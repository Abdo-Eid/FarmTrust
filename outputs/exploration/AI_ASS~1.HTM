Feature Design

AI Report Assistant

An AI that narrates and reasons over the land assessment — and can never change it.

Free to ask. Bounded in what it may assert.

Decision support — never a loan approval, rejection, or recommendation.
1

Downstream only

The AI reads the finished assessment; it never computes a status, score, or flag. Every number comes from the pipeline.

2

Works without AI

The report is complete and trustworthy with the assistant switched off. The AI is an optional layer with an automatic plain-text fallback — never load-bearing.

3

Every sentence sourced & typed

Each statement links to its evidence and carries a claim type: measureddeterministicuser / localhypothesisunknown

4

Narrate on demand · ask freely (bounded)

The AI narrates a report on request and answers open questions as a bounded analyst — it can offer alternative explanations and suggest what to inspect next. Users ask anything; the AI is constrained only in what it may assert.

5

Grounded in local context

It draws on a local-agronomy context layer (e.g. fodder-cutting patterns, rotation timing, pest invisibility) to avoid naive reads — treated as context, not automatic truth, with cautious wording.

6

Guardrails enforced, not requested

Forbidden claims are blocked in code and tested; generated text is validated against the evidence, and anything unsupported is rejected and replaced by the plain summary. The model must be unable to overstep, not merely told not to.

MAY What the AI is allowed to do

  • Narrate the assessment on request
  • Answer open questions as a bounded analyst
  • Explain why confidence is high or low
  • Offer alternative explanations — labelled as hypotheses
  • Use local context to inform its wording
  • Suggest what to inspect next
  • Tag every statement by claim type

NEVER What the AI must never do

  • Approve, reject, or recommend a loan
  • State yield, income, or price
  • Diagnose pests or disease
  • Assert crop type as fact
  • Claim anything not in the evidence, or leave it untyped
  • Override or change a pipeline result

Decision-support, not a loan decision.

The assistant lives beside the deterministic report, never in place of it. It narrates on request and takes open questions, but every line shows its claim type and its source, and unsupported answers fall back to the plain summary. A standing "decision-support, not a loan decision" note sits on every generated surface. Lender-first, English-first.
