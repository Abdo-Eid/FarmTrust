Section 6 — The AI assistant

It knows its limits.

Decision support, not a decision — it won't approve, reject, or score financing. A deterministic fallback keeps the pipeline the source of truth, and the hallucination risk stays stated, not hidden.

FarmTrust Assistantgpt-4o · grounded
So — should we approve this loan?
That's your call to make. I can lay out the land evidence and where it's uncertain, but I don't approve, reject, or score financing. Out of scope boundary · decision support only
